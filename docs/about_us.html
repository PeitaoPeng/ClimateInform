About ClimateInform

ClimateInform is an independent climate forecasting initiative based in Virginia, USA. Our mission is to provide reliable, scientifically grounded short‑term climate forecasts to support informed decision‑making across climate‑sensitive sectors.

Our Focus

ClimateInform focuses on developing scientifically grounded tools and methodologies that advance climate prediction and diagnostics. Our work includes:

  • Development of climate forecast tools
  • Application of machine learning to climate prediction
  • Predictability and prediction of monthly and seasonal climate
  • Attribution diagnostics of extreme climate anomalies
  • Forecast verification and skill assessment

Our Approach

ClimateInform integrates statistical models, dynamical model guidance, and hybrid methodologies to produce forecasts that are both scientifically rigorous and easy to interpret.

We emphasize transparency, reproducibility, and clarity in all our forecast products.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing clear, scientifically grounded climate forecast products that support informed decision‑making across society. Our forecasts help stakeholders in the energy industry, agriculture, water resources, the financial sector, and government agencies anticipate risks, plan operations, and respond effectively to evolving climate conditions.

About the Founder

Peitao Peng, Ph.D., is an independent climate scientist with more than 30 years of experience in climate dynamics, prediction, and diagnostic analysis. He served at NOAA’s Climate Prediction Center from 1996 to 2023, including over two decades as a federal meteorologist.

Dr. Peng has developed a wide range of forecast tools and verification systems used in both operational and research environments. His expertise spans subseasonal‑to‑seasonal prediction, climate diagnostics, statistical‑dynamical modeling, and applications of machine learning (ML) in climate forecasting.

Today, he continues to design, refine, and maintain the forecast products featured on ClimateInform.com, with a strong emphasis on clarity, accessibility, and scientific integrity.
