About ClimateInform

ClimateInform is an independent climate forecasting initiative based in Virginia, USA. Our mission is to provide reliable, scientifically grounded short‑term climate forecasts to support decision‑making in agriculture, energy, water resources, and public planning.

Our Focus

We specialize in:

  • Seasonal climate outlooks
  • Monthly climate predictions
  • Nino3.4 and ENSO‑related diagnostics
  • Forecast verification and skill assessment

Our Approach

ClimateInform integrates statistical models, dynamical model guidance, and hybrid methodologies to produce forecasts that are both scientifically rigorous and easy to interpret.

We emphasize transparency, reproducibility, and clarity in all our forecast products.

Our Commitment

We are committed to improving public understanding of climate variability and providing accessible tools for interpreting climate forecasts.
